### Therapeutic castor oil must be sourced hexane-free to avoid solvent residue

Tag: Castor oil extraction standard

When sourcing castor oil for any protocol, the extraction method must be hexane-free. Conventional castor oil extraction uses hexane as a solvent, and residual hexane in the final product could introduce a lipophilic toxin that interferes with cellular redox or mitochondrial function.

Key points:
- **Treatment specification.** Castor oil for therapeutic use must be verified hexane-free.
- **Sourcing action.** Check the extraction method when purchasing castor oil; standard commercial castor oil may contain hexane residue.
- **Practical meaning.** A supplier's label stating 'organic' does not guarantee hexane-free extraction; explicit verification is required.
