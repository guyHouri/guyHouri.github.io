Tag: Mechanism

- Triplet oxygen (pro): Oxygen in its ground state with two unpaired electrons in parallel spin configuration (S=1); paramagnetic and chemically restricted, allowing it to safely accept electrons from the electron transport chain without reacting violently with surrounding molecules
- Singlet oxygen (pro): Oxygen in an excited state where the two electrons have antiparallel spins (S=0); diamagnetic and highly reactive, unable to safely accept electrons and prone to attacking lipids and DNA
- Complex IV (pro): Cytochrome c oxidase, the final enzyme complex in the electron transport chain where oxygen accepts electrons and is reduced to water

- intersystem crossing (pro): A quantum transition in which an electron flips its spin from parallel to antiparallel, changing the molecule's electronic state; in this case, converting triplet oxygen to singlet oxygen
- cardiolipin (pro): A phospholipid found in the inner mitochondrial membrane; essential for electron transport chain function and vulnerable to singlet oxygen attack
- deuterium-depleted water (pro): Water with a lower ratio of deuterium to protium; produced by triplet oxygen reduction and essential for proper mitochondrial and cellular function
