### Cancer Cells DO Burn Fat — Three Studies the Keto-Crowd Skips

Tag: Tumor Metabolism

Direct rebuttal to the recurring '80% of cancer ATP is glucose/glutamine fermentation' claim from the Seyfried school. Kruse argues tumor mitochondria are functional and metabolically plastic — many aggressive cancers actively weaponize FAO. 'Starve the tumor with keto' is an oversimplification that can be clinically dangerous.

Key points:
- **CPT1A Overdrive.** Aggressive malignancies — prostate cancer, glioblastoma, leukemia, triple-negative breast — upregulate CPT1A, the rate-limiting enzyme that shuttles fatty acids into mitochondria for β-oxidation.
- **Lipid-Rich Microenvironments.** Tumors in the omentum or bone marrow completely reprogram to feed almost exclusively on free fatty acids — the keto narrative collapses for these tumor types.
- **OXPHOS Still Dominant.** Quantitative energetic mapping shows mitochondrial OXPHOS remains the dominant ATP source in most human cancers, operating alongside upregulated aerobic glycolysis.

### Why Blood NAD Tests Miss What's Happening Inside the Cell

Tag: Mitochondrial Bioenergetics

Charles Brenner's lab admits whole-blood NAD+ is largely unrelated to where NR and NMN therapeutically act. Kruse extends: the supplement industry has been monitoring the wrong compartment — blood is engineered to be a clean transit highway, not a tissue that takes up these molecules.

Key points:
- **Red Blood Cells block the pathway.** Erythrocytes lack a nucleus, synthesize NAD only from nicotinic acid, and have a hard genetic block on internalizing NMN/NR. They make up 99.97% of blood cellular volume.
- **White Blood Cells block it too.** Neutrophils and monocytes make NAD solely from nicotinamide; they lack expression of the NMRK1 / NMRK2 genes that would let them use NR.
- **Solid tissues are where it acts.** Skeletal muscle, heart, and damaged peripheral nerves aggressively spike NMRK2 expression — that's the actual NR uptake site in the body.
- **Trigger is damage, not aging.** NMRK2 activates when the tissue's internal water lattice undergoes dielectric collapse under nnEMF + blue-light stress, jamming the F0-F1 ATP synthase rotors — an emergency-repair pathway, not a longevity supplement target.

### Why Glycolysis Has 10 Steps, Not 1

Tag: Biochemistry

Classical biochemistry frames glycolysis (10 steps) and the TCA cycle (9 steps) as a convoluted way to extract ATP. Kruse reads them as a precision isotopic filtration grid — the multi-step length is the point, not a flaw.

Key points:
- **No incoherent flash.** A single-step cleavage of glucose would release a destructive burst of incoherent UPEs — literal 'matrix fire'. Stepwise breakdown keeps the energy release coherent.
- **Fractional isotope centrifuge.** The 10 + 9 step machinery progressively isolates light protium (¹H) for the respiratory chain while trapping and exhausting heavy deuterium before it reaches the inner mitochondrial matrix.
- **Boston banana failure.** Tropical, high-deuterium carbs eaten at high winter latitudes overwhelm the filter. Without local solar IR/UV to drive the cortisol awakening response, the cellular water lattice collapses → the 10-step filter can't keep up.

Concepts:
- glycolysis (pro): The 10-enzyme cytoplasmic pathway that breaks one glucose molecule into two pyruvate molecules, producing a net 2 ATP. The first stage of cellular respiration; classically taught as 'just ATP extraction'.
- TCA cycle (pro): Tricarboxylic Acid cycle (Krebs cycle, citric acid cycle) — the 9-enzyme mitochondrial loop that finishes breaking down acetyl-CoA, producing reduced electron carriers (NADH, FADH2) that feed the electron transport chain.
- UPE (pro): Ultra-weak Photon Emission — extremely faint biophoton light (single photons per cell per second) released by living tissue during oxidative metabolism. Kruse treats coherent UPEs as the cell's internal signaling channel and chaotic UPEs as damage signatures.
- protium (pro): The light isotope of hydrogen (¹H) — one proton, no neutrons. About 99.985% of all natural hydrogen. Mitochondria preferentially handle protium and try to exclude its heavy cousin deuterium.
- deuterium (noob): Heavy isotope of hydrogen (one extra neutron). Chemically like hydrogen but twice as massive — when it ends up inside mitochondrial ATP-synthase machinery it jams the rotor.
- cortisol awakening response (pro): The natural ~50% surge in blood cortisol within the first 30-45 minutes after waking, normally driven by morning sunlight on the retina. A signature circadian marker; blunted by indoor light and late screen exposure.

Concepts:
