### Solar exposure and grounding optimize collagen repair after surgery

Tag: Post-surgical wound healing

After surgery, the wound site requires three coordinated inputs: direct solar exposure to the healing area, sustained grounding contact, and metabolic support for triplet state oxygen and NAD+ recycling. Collagen is a chiral molecule, meaning its three-dimensional structure depends on the correct spin state of electrons during remodeling. Solar light and ground contact together support the redox environment needed for proper collagen alignment and cross-linking. This protocol applies immediately post-op and continues through the remodeling phase.

Source quote: "Collagen is a chiral molecule. Make sure the area is solar exposed post op and you are doing serious grounding for the wound to heal best. You need triplet state oxygen and and NAD+ recycling to accomplish this goal."

Concepts:
- collagen (noob): The primary structural protein in connective tissue, bone, and skin. After surgery, collagen must be remodeled and cross-linked to restore tissue strength and integrity.
- chiral molecule (noob): Collagen's biological function depends on its correct chiral orientation; improper orientation weakens tissue repair.
- triplet state oxygen (pro): Molecular oxygen in its ground state with three unpaired electrons, the form required for efficient energy transfer and collagen cross-linking in healing tissue. Solar exposure and grounding help maintain this oxygen state in wound tissue.
- NAD+ recycling (pro): The continuous regeneration of NAD+ (nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide), a critical electron carrier in mitochondrial energy production and redox-dependent repair pathways essential for collagen remodeling.
