Tag: Cancer apoptosis and UV biophoton signaling

Pro-apoptotic Bax/Bak proteins expose tryptophan (Trp) residues, enhancing UV emission (~340 - 350 nm), amplifying the death signal. Cancer is a reversion to pre-GOE (Warburg) metabolism, losing UV biophoton control. Decentralized medicine restores UV exposure and apoptotic capacity.
