Concepts

Triplet oxygen (pro)
The ground state of molecular oxygen with two unpaired electrons in parallel spin configuration (S=1). Paramagnetic and chemically restricted, it can only accept electrons in the mitochondrial electron transport chain through the spin-matching mechanism of CISS.
Singlet oxygen (pro)
An excited, unstable state of oxygen where the two valence electrons have antiparallel spins (S=0). Diamagnetic and hyper-reactive, it breaks orderly electron transport and causes mitochondrial dysfunction.
Cytochrome c Oxidase (pro)
Complex IV of the electron transport chain, the final step where electrons are transferred to oxygen. Contains a binuclear copper center that matches the spin of incoming electrons.
Spin-polarized electrons (pro)
Electrons whose spin orientation has been selected or aligned by the chiral geometry of the electron transport chain via CISS, ensuring they match the parallel spin configuration of triplet oxygen.
Chirality-Induced Spin Selectivity (CISS) (pro)
A quantum mechanical effect where the chiral structure of biological molecules (like proteins in the electron transport chain) selects electrons based on their spin orientation, filtering out electrons with mismatched spins.
Deuterium-depleted water (pro)
Water with a lower ratio of deuterium (heavy hydrogen) to protium (normal hydrogen) than naturally occurring water. Produced in mitochondria when triplet oxygen cleanly accepts electrons, this water supports optimal cellular function.
Planetary geodynamo (pro)
Earth's magnetic field generated by convection in the liquid outer core. This field stabilizes the spin configuration of triplet oxygen and enables proper electron transport in mitochondria.
Intersystem crossing (pro)
A quantum transition where an electron flips its spin from parallel to antiparallel alignment, converting triplet oxygen to singlet oxygen. Triggered by external magnetic field collapse.
Cardiolipin (pro)
A phospholipid found in the inner mitochondrial membrane that is essential for optimal electron transport chain function. Singlet oxygen damages cardiolipin, impairing mitochondrial respiration.
Mitochondrial DNA (pro)
Genetic material located in mitochondria that encodes proteins essential for energy production. Singlet oxygen damages mtDNA, further impairing mitochondrial function.
Iron-sulfur clusters (pro)
Prosthetic groups in electron transport chain proteins that facilitate electron transfer. Singlet oxygen oxidatively damages these clusters, stalling electron flow.
Landauer Liquidation (pro)
A thermodynamic process where information loss in a system is converted to heat. In cells, mitochondrial dysfunction triggers massive information loss and release of non-coherent infrared waste heat.
