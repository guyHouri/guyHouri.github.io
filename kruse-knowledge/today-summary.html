Concepts

DDW (noob)
Deuterium-depleted water; water with a lower concentration of deuterium (a heavy form of hydrogen) than normal water, used to reduce cellular deuterium burden.
glymphatic (pro)
The brain's waste-clearance system that uses cerebrospinal fluid (CSF) flow to remove metabolic byproducts, including excess deuterium, during sleep and rest states.
CSF (noob)
Cerebrospinal fluid; the clear fluid that surrounds the brain and spinal cord, supporting nutrient delivery and waste removal.
cribriform plate (pro)
A thin, perforated bone in the nasal cavity that separates the nasal passages from the brain; contains olfactory nerve fibers and serves as a potential cerebrospinal fluid exit point.
Z-axis (pro)
Kruse framework describing the brain-to-heart vector along which deuterium and other solutes are cleared from the central nervous system via glymphatic and cardiovascular pathways.
isotonic (noob)
A solution with the same osmotic pressure as body fluids, preventing water from flowing in or out of cells.
H/D exchange (pro)
The biochemical swap of hydrogen atoms with deuterium atoms; a key mechanism by which deuterium-depleted water may reduce cellular deuterium burden.
olfactory epithelium (pro)
The tissue lining the nasal cavity that detects odors and connects directly to the brain via the olfactory nerve; a potential route for local isotopic manipulation.
