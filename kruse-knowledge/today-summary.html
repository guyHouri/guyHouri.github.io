Concepts

GLP-1 receptor agonists (noob)
A class of diabetes and weight-loss drugs (such as semaglutide and tirzepatide) that activate GLP-1 receptors in the pancreas and brain; they slow stomach emptying and reduce appetite
CISS (pro)
Chirality-Induced Spin Selectivity; a quantum mechanism where chiral (asymmetric) molecules preferentially transmit electrons of one spin state over another, critical for mitochondrial electron transport
triplet oxygen (pro)
Ground-state molecular oxygen (³O₂) with two unpaired electrons in parallel spin configuration; the normal, less reactive form of oxygen in cells
singlet oxygen (pro)
Highly reactive form of oxygen (¹O₂) where two electrons have opposite spins; causes oxidative damage to lipids, proteins, and DNA
cardiolipin (pro)
A critical phospholipid found almost exclusively in the inner mitochondrial membrane; essential for optimal function of the electron transport chain and ATP synthesis
mitochondrial DNA (noob)
DNA contained within mitochondria (mtDNA); when leaked into the cytosol, it triggers inflammatory alarm systems
cGAS-STING (pro)
A cytosolic DNA-sensing pathway; when activated by leaked mtDNA, it triggers production of Type I Interferons and inflammatory responses
Type I Interferons (pro)
Signaling proteins released during viral infection or cellular stress; in excess, they create chronic inflammation and disrupt normal cellular communication
2 Hz neural integration frequency (pro)
A proposed master oscillation frequency in neural tissue that coordinates cellular signaling and stem cell regulation; disruption by inflammatory noise impairs regenerative capacity
ubiquitination (pro)
A cellular tagging process where ubiquitin proteins mark damaged or misfolded proteins for degradation; overload of this pathway indicates severe cellular stress
Landauer Liquidation (pro)
A thermodynamic principle where erasing corrupted information in a system generates waste heat (kB T ln 2); applied here to cellular protein misfolding and ubiquitination overload
EZ water (pro)
Exclusion zone water; highly ordered, structured water found near hydrophilic surfaces in cells; maintains cell potential and supports optimal mitochondrial function
dielectric constant (pro)
A measure of a material's ability to hold an electric charge; organized cellular water has a dielectric constant around 160, while bulk water is around 78
focal lattice (pro)
A localized crystalline or semi-crystalline structure in cellular water; when this structure collapses (focal lattice lock), water loses its organized properties and cellular function degrades
ATP (noob)
Adenosine triphosphate; the primary energy currency of cells, produced by mitochondria and used to power all cellular processes
